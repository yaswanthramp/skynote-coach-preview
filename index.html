skyNote Coach

Eleven screens covering FR-1 to FR-16 of the functional requirements: the private personal coach, the sales scorecard, the manager's coaching workflow, administration and mobile.

Built with the Radix design system, Skypoint amber, Inter. Every colour, size, radius and shadow is a token. The only component added is the range bar, derived from the same 12 step scale roles and documented in components-coach.md.
The method
See a number. Then hear yourself say it.

skyNote already listens to every meeting. Coach turns that recording into private, measured feedback, scored against a healthy range and backed by the exact seconds of the call where it happened. For sales teams the same engine grades calls against a rubric the team defines, so a manager can coach ten reps in the time it used to take to review one.

Design principles
See, then say

Every number is one click from the seconds of audio that produced it. A metric with no moment behind it is an opinion, and Coach never shows one.

A value is meaningless without its range

The signature component is a bar carrying four marks: the ideal band, your value, your 30 day baseline and, on team screens, the top performer band. Status is written in words as well as colour.

Private until proven otherwise

Personal Coach carries a lock and a neutral callout. Team data carries the blue users callout. The two are never mixed on one card, and the disclosure appears before the first metric exists.

Three recommendations, three goals

Fifteen metrics is a wall. The misses sort to the top, advice stops at three and goals stop at three. Everything else is available, nothing else is pushed.

Say why, in the user's words

Every failure state names its cause. Every AI grade carries one sentence of justification and the moments behind it, and a human override that wins.

Never a performance rating

No composite rep score, no ranking without an explicit metric, no HR surface. The manager's page states what it cannot show.

The decisions worth arguing about
Coach is a tab on the meeting, not a destination

The moment a person cares how they spoke is the moment they open the call. A separate Coach product gets visited on launch day and never again. My Coach exists for trends. The meeting tab is where the habit forms.

My Coach is the same for everyone. My Discovery only exists with a CRM

My Coach answers how you speak, which is the same question for a counselor and an engineer, so it never changes shape. My Discovery answers who you are speaking to, which only has an answer when a CRM is connected. Splitting on that line means nobody watches half of a screen disappear when an integration is switched off, and the CRM state is one attribute on the root element driving the nav item, the prospect column and the meeting panel together.

Six destinations, not seventeen

Sub screens are reached from their parent and highlight the parent nav item. Trends, one metric and goals are tabs inside My Coach. Reports, review, leaderboard and keywords are tabs inside Team Coaching. The sidebar stays a list of places, not a list of features.

The range bar is the one component the system did not already have

Everything else on these screens is a stock card, table, badge, callout, segmented control or progress bar. The range bar was derived from the same 12 step roles: gray-4 track, green-3 band on a green-7 hairline, red, orange or green marker, gray-9 baseline tick.

Amber stays brand, so status had somewhere to go

Out of range is red, edge of range is orange, in range is green, team data is blue. Amber is left for the active nav pill, the one solid CTA per view, links and the AI voice. A score is never painted amber, so amber never reads as data.

Suggested goals are easier than the ideal range

Coach suggests 3m 30s when the ideal is 3m 00s, then raises the target after five held meetings. A target you can hit this week beats a correct target you ignore.

Every AI grade shows its working and can be overruled

One sentence of justification, the moments that justify it, and an override that wins and is attributed. Without those three, nobody trusts a graded call twice.

How the open questions are handled
Reporting relationships, open question 1

Team Coaching assumes a manager to direct report edge exists. The design shows six reports and states the rule in the blue callout. Wherever that edge comes from, the interface does not change.

Default ranges, open question 2

Ranges are editable everywhere and Settings keeps the shipped default beside any edited value, so validating them against the corpus later costs nothing in the interface.

Sales tier packaging, open question 3

Scorecards, leaderboards, the clip library and keyword tracking are independently switchable modules, so a paid add on is a flag change rather than a redesign.

Non English tuning, open question 4

Filler lists and wpm bands are workspace editable in Settings. A per language set is the same screen scoped differently.

Interruption source, open question 5

The interruption metric states its source and a confidence label, high, from timeline events rather than transcript overlap, which works whichever source ships.

Uploads and browser recordings, open question 6

Listed explicitly in the Settings exclusion list and marked pending, so the decision has a home in the interface either way.

Out of scope, deliberately. No real time in call nudges, Coach is post meeting in v1 to v3 and the mobile capture panel says so. No role play or practice simulator. No model other than Gemini. No performance management surface: there is no composite rep rating anywhere in this set, and the manager's page states what it cannot show.
